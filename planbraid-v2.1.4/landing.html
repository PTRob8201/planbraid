Continuum

See the bigger picture.

Bring projects together in one continuous outline. Edit notes in place or open a project when you want to focus.

  • Browse projects across spaces
  • Expand or collapse nested notes
  • Hide completed notes when you need a clearer view

Good to know

A little more detail.

How do I access PlanBraid?

Access is by invitation only. An administrator creates accounts and can help with password resets.

Can I export my work?

Yes. You can export a workspace backup or export a visible outline as text, Markdown or PDF.

Can I share a project with another account?

Each account has a separate workspace. Project sharing and in-app project invitations are not currently available.
